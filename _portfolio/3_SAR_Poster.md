I developed an automated flood mapping workflow using Sentinel-1 Synthetic Aperture Radar (SAR) within Google Earth Engine to rapidly identify inundation extents during disaster events when optical imagery is obscured by cloud cover. My approach combines multiband SAR polarization composites with adaptive speckle filtering and depth-based pixel removal to improve flood delineation accuracy while minimizing false positives in urban areas. The system eliminates the need for hand-labeled training data by leveraging unsupervised classification, achieving nearly 90% accuracy and outperforming traditional supervised methods. Designed for scalability, the workflow can be quickly adapted to any region, enabling emergency managers and researchers to generate reliable flood maps in near real time.
